\chapter{Additional Completion-Strategy Comparisons}
\label{app:completion-comparisons}

These figures complement the Intel verbs comparisons in
\autoref{sec:completion-methods}. All use the two-node, two-rank
\texttt{cm4\_inter} configuration and the MPI \texttt{seq/seq-seq} policy.
Solid circles denote \texttt{wait\_all}, and dashed orange diamonds denote
\texttt{test\_all}. Case~01/02 disables progress; Case~03/04 enables the
implementation-specific progress configuration. Points show the mean
normalized wait reduction across experimental runs. These plots show means,
not confidence intervals, and do not establish equivalence of the completion
methods.
